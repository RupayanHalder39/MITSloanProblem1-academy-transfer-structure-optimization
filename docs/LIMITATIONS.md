# Limitations

- All reported economic values are modelled; they are not realised transfer
  proceeds, accounting profit, or return on investment.
- Market value is not transfer fee.
- The buyer ceiling and buyer acceptance are modelled rather than observed.
- The study does not recover historically optimal contracts or prove that the
  selected structures would have been agreed.
- Buyer behaviour is not directly observed.
- Clause enforceability, contract semantics, payment timing, and realised
  retained-right payouts are not available as a complete validation ledger.
- The three-year forecast remains uncertain, particularly for lower-value
  players.
- The 36-exit optimization is observational and simulation-based. It does not
  support causal claims.
- The seven nominal retention selections are numerical ties with full sale,
  add €0 modelled value, and lack workable buyer-seller overlap.
- End-to-end independent reproduction requires lawful access to source data
  that cannot be redistributed here.

Stronger validation requires observed contract terms, buyer behaviour, clause
enforcement, and realised payouts on genuinely out-of-sample decisions.
